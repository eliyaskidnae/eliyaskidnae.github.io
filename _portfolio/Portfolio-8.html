" collection: portfolio --- Automated sorting and pick-and-place are fundamental tasks in industrial robotics, requiring precise motion control, repeatable positioning, and reliable object handling. This project develops an automated sorting system using a Stäubli TS60 SCARA robot to identify, pick, transport, and place metallic and plastic cylinders into their designated locations. The system executes coordinated pick-and-place sequences while maintaining accurate robot positioning and consistent manipulation throughout the sorting process. The project demonstrates industrial robotic manipulation, motion sequencing, and automated material handling using a SCARA robotic platform.
